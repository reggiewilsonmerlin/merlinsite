Our Story

A Family Passion Since 1977

Based near Stockport, our family has been in the classic and vintage vehicle restoration business since before we ever opened our doors commercially. We are a small, dedicated family business — established formally in 1983 — with a pedigree stretching back to 1977.

We are renowned Austin Healey restoration specialists, but our expertise extends to virtually every British and European classic. No vehicle is too rare or unusual — we have helped produce a one-off Delage D8S, one of only two remaining in the world, and the Austin Healey BN3 now housed in the Austin Healey Museum.
