Most Popular Service

Servicing & Ongoing Maintenance

Keeping your classic in perfect running order doesn't have to mean a full restoration. Regular, expert servicing is the single most important thing you can do for a classic or vintage vehicle — and it's far more affordable than you might think.

Our workshop team has decades of hands-on experience with the quirks and demands of classic vehicles — from Healey carburettors to Jaguar cooling systems. We treat every car as if it were our own.

Routine servicing & oil changes
MOT preparation & testing
Brake inspection, adjustment & overhaul
Carburettor cleaning, balancing & tuning
Cooling system service & repair
Electrical diagnosis & repair
Suspension, steering & wheel alignment
Fuel system maintenance
Seasonal preparation & storage checks

Full Restoration

From Strip-Down to Concours Finish

A complete restoration is the ultimate investment in a classic car. Our team has completed hundreds of full restorations — from bare-metal resprays to total ground-up rebuilds — each documented with a full photographic record that becomes part of your vehicle's history file.

We are multiple concours d'elegance award winners, and approach every restoration with the same obsessive attention to detail — whether it's destined for shows or the open road.

Full bare-metal strip and inspection
Chassis repair, restoration & fabrication
Complete body rebuild to original specification
Factory-correct paint finishes
Full mechanical rebuild
Interior trimming & upholstery
Full photographic documentation throughout

Bodywork & Paintwork

Expert Panel Work & Finishing

Our bodywork and paintwork capability is one of our greatest strengths. From minor dents and rust repairs to complete bodyshell fabrication, we have the skills and equipment to handle any challenge.

We have produced everything from factory-correct period colours to bespoke custom finishes, including bare-metal work and House of Kolor chameleon paint. Every panel that leaves our workshop meets our exacting standards.
